Nature Facts

A few surprising truths about the living world.

  • Trees can communicate through underground fungal networks.
  • A single mature tree can absorb over 20 kg of CO2 each year.
  • The ocean holds about 97% of all water on Earth.
  • Bees pollinate roughly one third of the food we eat.
  • Mountains cover about 24% of the planet's land surface.
  • Some bamboo species grow up to 90 cm in a single day.
